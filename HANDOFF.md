## Start here

Open `project.godot` in Godot. The configured entry point is `game/scenes/main.tscn`. `game/main.tscn` wraps it. Prior checks used Godot 4.7.2 stable, Compatibility renderer, on Windows. Start with the scene Inspector: instance and inherited-scene overrides can supersede script defaults. The older README still describes an arm rig and is stale in places.

DARKSOLES is an original portrait mobile endless dungeon crawler rebuilt in 3D from the owner's `DARKSOLES PROTOTYPE.html` (originally in Downloads). It should feel dark, confined and readable in motion. Near surfaces, turns and enemies need to read in false torchlight. The visual references were for mood; there is no card interface. The owner wants reusable Godot scenes, visible assets on the nodes they edit, and tunable values exposed in the Inspector.

## Run, dungeon and encounters

- `game/gameplay/run.gd` coordinates run state, movement, encounters, damage, pickups, dialogue, transitions, and HUD refresh. Best distance persists in `user://descent.cfg`. Title fades through black into play. Death shows distance, kills and best descent.
- `game/world/dungeon.gd` streams modular corridor scenes ahead of the player. The Dungeon node on the main scene exposes generation distances, encounter weights, junction and stair settings. `corridor.tscn` and its script assemble decks, arches, branch walls, doors and flights. Doors use multiple variants and outlines. Stairs only descend, occur occasionally (including behind doors), tip the camera down and exclude enemy encounters.
- The player moves forward automatically. Tap left, center or right at a branching path. There are no directional buttons. The camera turns 90 degrees. A top prompt announces the branch. Route hints suggest treasure/light or enemy shadows. Junction slowdown affects cooldown progression, animation and SFX. Player encounter distance is the exported `enemy_encounter_range` on the run controller.
- Enemy scenes in `game/enemies/` inherit `enemy_base.tscn`. Skeleton, wraith, slime, bandit, spider and undead have individual spawn weights. Slimes are rarer and can ambush the player when a chest opens. Each enemy's Illustration node has its own material with its sprite assigned directly. Edit its transform for scale/framing and its shader's illustration parameter for art. Bandit uses Thief, spider uses Tarantula, undead uses Weird Humanoid. Slime has extra squash animation. Enemy health, windups, recovery, damage, audio and conversation settings belong on the enemy.
- Enemy windups use timing rings. Heavy hits break a raised shield; timed parries can stun. Attacks land on impact cues. Low health is a dark pulsing dithered red vignette rather than a meter. Losing the left hand disables that hand's equipment/actions. There are no visible arms.

## Weapons and loot

- `game/player/player.tscn` is the first-person camera and player tuning scene. **Current scene overrides start the bar with Shield / Slash / Shield Parry.** The scene also sets hit stop to zero, although the system supports adjustable hit stop. Check scene overrides before changing defaults in `player.gd`.
- Open AnimatedWeapons in the player scene to edit RightHand and LeftHand. Both use `weapon_rig.tscn`. Art source is `art/models/weapons.blend`; runtime imports `weapons_runtime.glb`. Separate sword/shield armatures, Blender camera framing, idle clips and per-hand animation playback support mirrored dual wielding. `weapon_rig.gd` filters imported tracks, maps action clips and inserts timed damage events. Preserve draw → sword to idle → shield to idle, return clips after actions, synchronized walking sway, and sword-parry depth in front of the shield. The live weapon setup uses the animated rigs. Older sword/shield/tome scenes exist in the current checkout; their presence does not make them equipped. Fireball has an active projectile scene.
- Exactly **three action slots**, across two hands. Slot ownership follows the hand, independent of button position. Slash, Shield, Shield Bash, Sword Parry and Shield Parry are distinct IDs. Both parries display “Parry” on the bar with different icons. Other supported actions include Fireball and Mend.
- Treasure prompt at top, choices below. Opening can reveal a rotating, outlined weapon or trigger a slime. Weapon flow in `run.gd`: `weapon_offer` (Take/Discard), `weapon_hand` (Left/Right and small Back), `weapon_actions` (multi-select, Confirm appears once something is selected), `weapon_slots` (place each action). Replacing a hand clears its old actions. The model changes after the last slot assignment. Dual swords and dual shields are supported. `game/ui/weapon_preview.gd` renders the rotating mesh in a transparent SubViewport.

## Talk

Talk appears during a fight. It replaces the three combat actions with Plead / Reason / Threaten. The opening and reply type out at the top, combat rings disappear, and Back is available before committing. Each enemy allows one committed attempt. A peaceful outcome clears the encounter without a kill. The enemy dissolves during the final 0.7 seconds of the farewell. Failure provokes three faster heavy attacks: parryable, shield-breaking. Default timing is 0.25 s before windup, 0.7 s windup, 0.45 s between swings.

Damage or stun marks the enemy as previously attacked. Each profile has greeting, odds and outcome lines for untouched and wounded states. Response arrays and chance vectors follow Plead / Reason / Threaten order. Bandits have authored dialogue and favor Reason before a hit (80%, falling to 35% after a hit). Threaten becomes more convincing after drawing blood. Other current enemies inherit a profile with zero chance for Reason. Edit these in the enemy Inspector. **Deferred idea:** bribery for suitable enemies once currency exists. No currency or bribe flow is implemented.

## Presentation, audio and performance

- `game/ui/hud.tscn` and `hud.gd` manage title, combat, dialogue, treasure, loot and death. Edit `bottom_row.tscn` once to change the layout across screens. Its HBox gives two or three visible actions equal width. `action_button.tscn` standardizes press behavior, including Talk/Back/Confirm. Buttons are pure black with light borders. Font is Beholden. Use Godot button signals; a manual touch-release handler previously toggled loot selections twice. Project touch-to-mouse emulation is currently **false** for mobile testing. The owner's mobile editor also has its own touch simulation setting.
- `game/rendering/shaders/` contains the world dither, final palette, gradient torchlight, outline passes, enemy illustration, door/floor finishes and hurt vignette. Palette is black/white/red. The ceiling recedes into black; preserve close-range floor texture and silhouettes. The full-screen dither, palette and vignette share `dither_grid.gdshaderinc` so dots occupy whole display pixels. Project uses canvas-items stretch with a 540 × 1170 design size.
- `game/audio/game_audio.gd` handles Mistridden Woods on title, Necron's Tower during gameplay, crossfade, footsteps, draw/swing, material-specific impacts, parry, doors, chests and other events. Some samples come from the owner's trimmed “from freesound” folder. Door sound follows opening and varies pitch. SFX respond to slowdown. Enemy impact tags and optional overrides distinguish bone, flesh, slime, shield and other materials. Check main scene Audio overrides for effective levels.
- `game/world/static_mesh_batch.gd` batches repeated tread, pillar and sconce drip meshes at runtime while keeping the source nodes editable. Measured isolated desktop draw calls went from 16 to 7 for a stair flight with sconce and 3 to 2 for an arch. Those are module counts, not a phone frame-rate benchmark.
